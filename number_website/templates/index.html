📚 About Number Properties

  • Prime Numbers: Numbers greater than 1 that have no divisors other than 1 and themselves (e.g., 2, 3, 5, 7, 11)
  • Perfect Numbers: Numbers equal to the sum of their proper divisors (e.g., 6 = 1 + 2 + 3)
  • Armstrong Numbers: Numbers equal to the sum of their digits raised to the power of the number of digits (e.g., 153 = 1³ + 5³ + 3³)
  • Fibonacci Sequence: A sequence where each number is the sum of the two preceding ones (0, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13...)
